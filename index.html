Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How long does it take to distribute my music? +

Typically 2-5 business days. Once approved, your music is sent to all major platforms and becomes available to listeners worldwide.

Do you take a commission on my earnings? +

No! We charge a one-time fee per release. You keep 100% of your streaming revenue. That's our promise to independent artists.

Can I upload any type of music? +

Yes! We support all genres. Whether it's hip-hop, afrobeats, reggae, pop, electronic, or any other genre, we'll get it distributed.

What happens after my music is released? +

Your music stays live on platforms forever. You get access to our dashboard to track streams, earnings, and listener demographics across all platforms.

Do you provide customer support? +

Absolutely! We offer 24/7 customer support via email. Our team is dedicated to helping you succeed as an artist.
