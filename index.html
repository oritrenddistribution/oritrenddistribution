Frequently Asked Questions

When can I start uploading my songs? ⌄
Immediately after subscription! You'll get instant access to your personal artist dashboard. No waiting for approval. Upload your songs directly and start distributing right away.
How quickly will my music be distributed to streaming platforms? ⌄
Your music will typically be live within 2-5 business days on most platforms. Spotify and Apple Music may take up to 2 weeks for official playlist consideration, but your music will be available for streaming immediately.
Do you take a percentage of my streaming revenue? ⌄
No! We never take a commission on your earnings. We charge a yearly subscription fee, and 100% of your streaming revenue from Spotify, Apple Music, and all other platforms goes directly to you. What you earn is what you keep!
Can I edit my song information after I submit it? ⌄
Yes! You can edit your song title, artist name, artwork, features, lyrics, and other metadata anytime through your personal dashboard before your music goes live. Once it's live on streaming platforms, some changes may require resubmission.
Is there a limit to how many songs I can distribute? ⌄
It depends on your plan! The Artist plan allows up to 5 tracks per year. The Pro plan has unlimited releases with no restrictions. The Label plan also offers unlimited releases. You can upload and manage as many songs as you want on Pro and Label plans!
What happens if my music violates copyright or platform policies? ⌄
We thoroughly review all submissions before distribution. If there are any copyright concerns or policy violations, we'll notify you immediately through your dashboard and work with you to resolve them. Our 24/7 support team is here to help!
Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription plan? ⌄
Yes! You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time to access more features. Contact our support team for downgrade requests and we'll work with you.
