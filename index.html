Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will my music be distributed to streaming platforms? ⌄
Your music will typically be live within 2-5 business days on most platforms. Spotify and Apple Music may take up to 2 weeks for official playlist consideration, but your music will be available for listeners immediately. We expedite the process as much as possible to get your work in front of your audience quickly.
Do you take a percentage of my streaming revenue? ⌄
No! We never take a commission on your earnings. We charge a one-time distribution fee, and 100% of your streaming revenue from Spotify, Apple Music, and all other platforms goes directly to you. What you earn is yours to keep.
Can I edit my song information after I submit it? ⌄
Yes! You can edit your song title, artist name, artwork, features, lyrics, and other metadata anytime before your music goes live. Once it's live on streaming platforms, some changes may take time to update across all services, but we'll help you manage this process smoothly.
Is there a limit to how many songs I can distribute? ⌄
It depends on your plan! The Starter plan allows 1 single per submission. The Artist plan allows up to 5 tracks. The Pro and Label plans have unlimited releases with no restrictions. You can distribute as much as you want, whenever you want.
What happens if my music violates copyright or platform policies? ⌄
We thoroughly review all submissions before distribution. If there are any copyright concerns or policy violations, we'll notify you immediately and work with you to resolve them. Our 24/7 support team provides guidance on proper metadata, rights clearance, and content policies to ensure your music gets approved.
