Frequently Asked Questions

When can I start uploading my songs? ⌄
Immediately after subscription! You'll get instant access to your personal artist dashboard. No waiting for approval. Upload your songs directly and start distributing right away.
How quickly will my music be distributed to streaming platforms? ⌄
Your music will typically be live within 2-5 business days on most platforms. Spotify and Apple Music may take up to 2 weeks for official playlist consideration, but your music will be available for streaming immediately.
Do you take a percentage of my streaming revenue? ⌄
No! We never take a commission on your earnings. We charge a yearly subscription fee, and 100% of your streaming revenue from Spotify, Apple Music, and all other platforms goes directly to your account.
Can I edit my music details after uploading? ⌄
Yes! You can edit metadata (title, artist name, artwork, lyrics, etc.) anytime before your release goes live. Once live on platforms, some details may require platform-specific updates.
What happens to my music if I cancel my subscription? ⌄
Your music stays live on all platforms forever! Canceling your subscription only stops you from uploading new music. All previously distributed music remains available and continues to earn you revenue.
Do you offer support for music metadata and artwork? ⌄
Absolutely! Our 24/7 support team helps with metadata updates, artwork specifications, playlist pitching, and any technical questions. We're here to help your music succeed.
