About Oritrend Distribution

Our Mission

To democratize music distribution and empower independent artists worldwide by providing fast, affordable, and transparent access to global streaming platforms.

Vision: Every artist, regardless of background or budget, deserves a platform to reach millions of listeners globally.

Who We Are

Oritrend Distribution is a modern music distribution platform built by music enthusiasts for independent artists. We understand the challenges artists face in getting their music heard, and we've created a solution that removes all the barriers.

Our platform connects artists directly with over 150 countries and 50+ streaming platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, YouTube Music, Boomplay, and Audiomack—all with zero commission on your earnings.
