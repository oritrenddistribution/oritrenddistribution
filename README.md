## ❓ FAQ

**How long does it take for my music to go live?**  
Typically 2-5 business days. Spotify and Apple Music may take up to 2 weeks for playlist consideration.

**Do you take a percentage of my earnings?**  
No! We charge a one-time distribution fee only. 100% of your streaming revenue is yours.

**Can I edit my music information after submission?**  
Yes! You can update artwork, title, and metadata until your music is live.

**Is there a limit to how many songs I can distribute?**  
No limits on the Pro and Label plans. The Starter plan allows 1 single per submission.

**What if my music gets removed from platforms?**  
We provide full support and work with platforms to resolve any copyright or content issues.
